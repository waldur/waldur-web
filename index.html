Open-Source Cloud and HPC Platform

One self-service marketplace for private clouds, public clouds, HPC and AI services.
Identity, billing, quotas and support desk are built in.
View demo Installation guide
Waldur Demo

Overview

Service marketplace

Providers publish offerings with price plans. Customers order them, with optional approval by the project, organization or provider.

Self-service portal

One portal with separate workspaces for end users, service providers and staff. Users sign in with their institutional or national identity.

Reports and dashboards

Usage, cost, revenue and capacity reports for staff, providers and organization owners, all exportable. Metrics can also be sent to Prometheus and Grafana.

Accounting and billing

Fixed-price, usage-based, one-time and limit-based pricing, with automatic invoices or export to external billing systems.

Service desk

Built-in support tickets, or a connection to Jira Service Management, Zammad or OpenText SMAX, so customers have one support line for every service.

Open source

MIT-licensed. Deploy with Helm or Docker Compose, on premises or in the cloud, with professional support available.

Features

One catalog for every backend

Offer private and public clouds, HPC clusters, Kubernetes platforms, storage and AI gateways side by side in one marketplace.
See all integrations

Teams, projects and roles

Customers invite people into projects, delegate resource management with role-based access and let roles expire automatically. Every change is recorded in an audit log.

Policies and budgets

Set cost and usage limits per organization, project or offering. When a limit is reached, Waldur notifies the team, blocks new orders or requests downscaling.
Learn more

Calls for proposals

Hand out resources through calls: application forms, review rounds, conflict-of-interest checks and allocation decisions. Accepted proposals become projects with the awarded resources.
Learn more

AI assistant

A built-in assistant helps users in plain language: finding open calls, preparing proposals, managing resources and finding their way around the portal.
Learn more

Project chat on Matrix

Every project gets an end-to-end encrypted Matrix room. Members join automatically based on their roles, and a Waldur bot answers questions about status, orders and members and opens support tickets from the chat.
Learn more

Automation and APIs

Everything in the portal is available through the REST API, with a Terraform provider, an Ansible collection and SDKs for Python and TypeScript.
Learn more

Credentials stay with the provider

Providers can connect their systems through the Waldur Site Agent, which runs on their side and keeps infrastructure credentials there.
Learn more

Federation

Independent Waldur deployments can share offerings, so partners use each other’s services while each keeps its own governance.

How it works

Waldur sits between the people who use cloud, HPC and AI services and the infrastructure that runs them: one portal and API in front, every provider's services behind it.

  1. Providers connect their infrastructure

    OpenStack, VMware vCenter, Rancher and other Waldur deployments connect through their APIs. Slurm and MOAB clusters, Kubernetes, OpenNebula, storage and AI gateways connect through the Site Agent. Each provider then publishes offerings with their prices.

  2. Users order from one catalog

    People sign in with their institutional or national identity and order an offering for a project. An order can wait for approval by the project, the organization or the provider, or resources can be awarded through a call for proposals.

  3. Waldur runs it and keeps the books

    Waldur provisions the resource, keeps the project's members in sync on the backend and collects usage. Usage turns into monthly invoices, and policies warn the team, block new orders or request downscaling when a budget runs out.

Under the hood: a Django backend and a React portal, with PostgreSQL and RabbitMQ, deployed with Helm on Kubernetes or with Docker Compose on a single machine.
