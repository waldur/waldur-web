Solutions

Waldur is used by organizations that provide computing services to many users. Each page describes the setting, how Waldur fits into it, what it leaves to other systems and how a rollout usually goes.

Research and AI infrastructure

HPC centers

Self-service access to Slurm and MOAB clusters and private clouds, allocation through requests or calls for proposals, and usage per project.

More on HPC centers

AI factories

Inference services and GPU capacity on OpenNebula, OpenStack or Kubernetes, for onboarded companies and researchers, paid or funded.

More on AI factories

Peer review portal

Calls for proposals with review workflows, reviewer assignment and conflict-of-interest checks, ending in allocations on the providers' systems.

More on peer review

NRENs

One catalog for the network's own services and those its member institutions provide, with eduGAIN sign-in.

More on NRENs

Service providers

Cloud and GPU providers

A customer portal, metering and invoicing for IaaS, GPU and LLM services on OpenStack, VMware, OpenNebula, Kubernetes and Slurm.

More on cloud and GPU providers

Public sector

Government cloud

Self-service for ministries and agencies on a cloud run by a central IT authority, with costs per agency and national sign-in.

More on government clouds

Digital marketplace

A catalog where vetted providers offer services to public bodies, with ordering, provisioning and invoicing online.

More on digital marketplaces

Enterprise IT

Self-service for internal IT

A catalog of IT services for company departments, with requests handled in the service desk the IT team already uses.

More on internal IT
