Self-service for Internal IT

Overview

IT departments provide servers, storage, software and support to the rest of the company. Waldur gives them a catalog where departments order these services. Each request is either provisioned automatically or turned into a ticket in the service desk the IT team already uses, so its back-office processes stay as they are.

  • A catalog of IT services: virtual machines on a private cloud such as OpenStack, VMware vCenter or OpenNebula, provisioned automatically, next to services fulfilled by the IT team.
  • Requests as tickets in Jira Service Management, Zammad, OpenText SMAX or Waldur's built-in service desk.
  • Company sign-in through Microsoft Entra ID, LDAP or Active Directory, Keycloak or SAML.
  • Approval of orders by project and department managers.
  • Costs per department and project, for internal chargeback.

In the portal

Recorded on a demo deployment: the organizations, people and numbers are made up.

Implementation

A rollout usually starts with a handful of services and the service desk, so that nothing changes for the IT team’s back office.

Project phases

  1. Proof of concept

    Deploy Waldur and list the first services, automated or fulfilled by the IT team.

  2. Service desk

    Connect Waldur to the service desk, so customers have one support channel.

  3. Company sign-in

    Connect Microsoft Entra ID, LDAP or SAML, so employees sign in with their usual account.

  4. Production launch

    Open the catalog to all departments. Set up monitoring and backups.
